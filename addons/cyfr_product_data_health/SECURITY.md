# Security and privacy review

The addon makes no external requests, sends no telemetry, stores no credentials, and does not change product or fiscal data. Scan and issue rows contain product links, company, policy, issue codes and timestamps. They inherit the database's normal backup and retention policy. Managers can delete historical scans and their issues.

`base.group_user` can read policies, its own scans and current issues; `product.group_product_manager` can change policies, create/run scans and see company history. Managers may delete a completed, non-current scan through its explicit retention action. Direct scan or issue writes and deletes are denied. Odoo 19 grants read access to product variants and templates to `base.group_user`, so this matches the core product-read audience. Global record rules restrict policies to global or allowed companies, and scans to allowed companies; group rules make scans owner-only for ordinary users. Issue rules additionally restrict the linked product's current template company and let ordinary users see only current issues.

The issue model adds the caller's accessible `product.product` query to every normal search. It also checks linked-product read access for direct record reads, formatted search results and exports. This closes the stale-snapshot case where a custom product rule changes after scanning. The implementation uses the private Odoo 19 `_search` / `_check_access` hooks; reverify these hooks before upgrading to a later Odoo major version. It does not materialize all product IDs.

The scan method checks manager membership and active-company access and scans products as that manager. It takes a row lock before each batch. Only the method uses `sudo()` to write its protected scan counters/state and derived issue rows; it never uses `sudo()` to select products. Direct RPC cannot set scan state/owner/counters or create/change/delete issues. A database uniqueness constraint prevents a product appearing twice in one scan.

The issue list uses normal Odoo export permissions. A product may be reassigned to another company after scanning; the issue rule evaluates the product's current company, hiding that row from users who no longer have access. Managers should delete obsolete snapshots under their data retention policy.

Open risks to verify per deployment: custom third-party product ACLs/rules can further restrict visible products and require a deployment-specific check. The scan is intentionally bounded at 200 products per click and can take several clicks on a large catalog. A simultaneous batch on the same scan is rejected with Odoo's lock error and can be retried. Separate scans completing for one company serialize on the company row; if one is busy, the other returns a retryable lock error. Coordinate scans operationally.
