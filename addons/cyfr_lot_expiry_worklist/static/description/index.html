Lot Expiry Worklist

Assign and acknowledge expiring stock by lot, exact internal location and stock owner in Odoo 19.

  • Bounded, on-demand scan of physical stock and native lot expiration dates.
  • Separate rows for locations and consigned owners, with on-hand, reserved and physical unreserved quantities.
  • Manager assignment, responsible-user acknowledgment and an action history that remains after stock leaves.
  • Direct links to native Odoo lot and quant screens. No stock or expiry-date changes.
