# Security and data handling

`stock.group_stock_user` can read worklist rows in active companies. Only `stock.group_stock_manager` can open scan and assignment wizards. Worklist rows grant no direct create/write/delete ACL to either role. Public scan, assignment and acknowledgment methods recheck native role, active company, linked-record read access and, for acknowledgment, assignee identity before a narrowly scoped administrative write. A manager may acknowledge an assigned row. Direct RPC writes to status, quantity, dates or assignee through the row model fail for normal users.

The company rule confines rows to active companies. Search, direct read, pivot and export also enforce current native lot, product, location and stock-owner visibility, including after a linked record's access changes. A bounded preflight compares matching quant and prior-action IDs under the caller's permissions with all matching records in the selected active company; a hidden record aborts the scan before any snapshot write. Scans also fail if a matching position references a lot, product, location or owner the caller cannot read. The error does not reveal which record or threshold is hidden. Companyless locations are not assigned to a company and are excluded. Owners remain separate; a consigned position cannot be mixed into unowned stock.

The addon stores a location-level quantity snapshot, dates, assignee, next action, note, acknowledgment user/time and Odoo chatter. It stores no attachments or external telemetry. Scans never call stock move, scrap, inventory adjustment, reservation or lot date writes. Snapshots are kept indefinitely until an administrator performs an explicit database retention process; uninstall removes the addon models and their records. There is no automatic purge or background job. Chatter follows Odoo's native model access controls. Do not put personal or sensitive customer information in action notes.

Known boundary: a stock user with access to a company's native lots/locations may read that company's worklist metadata across its locations; there is no custom per-location entitlement layer. A user who loses an active company or linked-record access cannot obtain its rows through list, direct read, export or action methods. Enterprise-specific security integration was not tested.
